    What the tests prove
    Consent lifecycle
    Grant, withdrawal, re-consent, purpose isolation, and stale notice denial.
    Request boundary
    Only the matching consumer and purpose can request allowed fields via READ; extra fields or WRITE operations are denied without a payload.
    Revocation orchestration
    Withdrawal cancels matching queued work, creates a pending job, delivers to the adapter, records honest failures, supports idempotent retries, and generates verifiable receipts.
    Tamper-evident ledger
    Recomputing every SHA-256 link detects modified, deleted, or reordered records. This demonstrates tamper-evidence, not legal compliance certification.

    Automated evidence proves prototype behavior only. It does not establish legal compliance or production security.
